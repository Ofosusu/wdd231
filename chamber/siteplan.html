Discover Ghana Website Plan

WDD 231 Individual Project

1. Site Name

Discover Ghana

The name "Discover Ghana" was selected because it clearly communicates the purpose of the website. Visitors can discover beautiful tourist attractions, historical landmarks, waterfalls, beaches, national parks, and cultural destinations across Ghana. The name is short, memorable, and suitable for tourism branding.

Suggested Domain: discoverghana.org

2. Site Purpose

The Discover Ghana website promotes tourism by providing visitors with information about some of Ghana's most popular tourist attractions. Users can explore destinations, view images, read descriptions, locate attractions, and follow links to learn more before planning a visit.

3. Scenarios

  • Which tourist attractions in Ghana are best for family vacations?
  • Where can I find waterfalls, beaches, and national parks to visit?
  • How can I learn more about a tourist attraction before planning my trip?
  • Which historical places should every visitor explore while visiting Ghana?
