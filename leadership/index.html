About This Project

Africa's struggle for independence transformed the continent forever. Throughout the twentieth century courageous leaders united their people against colonial rule, calling for freedom, dignity, equality, and self-government.

This website introduces several influential leaders who inspired independence movements and promoted African unity. Visitors can learn about their backgrounds, major achievements, challenges they faced, and the lasting impact they continue to have on African history today.

Important Dates

1957

Ghana becomes the first sub-Saharan African nation to gain independence.

1960

"The Year of Africa" as seventeen African nations achieve independence.

1963

Formation of the Organization of African Unity.

1983

Thomas Sankara becomes President of Burkina Faso.
