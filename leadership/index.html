About This Project

Africa's struggle for independence transformed the continent forever. Throughout the twentieth century courageous leaders united their people against colonial rule, calling for freedom, dignity, equality, and self-government.

This website introduces influential leaders who inspired independence movements and promoted African unity. Visitors can learn about their backgrounds, achievements, challenges, and lasting impact.

A Journey Through Time

Important Dates

1957

Ghana's Independence

Ghana became the first sub-Saharan African nation to gain independence from British colonial rule.

1960

The Year of Africa

Seventeen African nations achieved independence during a historic wave of decolonization.

1963

Organization of African Unity

Independent African states established the Organization of African Unity to strengthen continental cooperation.

1983

Sankara's Leadership

Thomas Sankara became President of Burkina Faso and introduced ambitious social and economic reforms.
